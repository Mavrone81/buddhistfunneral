Honouring The Dharma

Buddhist funeral rites & traditions

Each rite carries deep meaning — generating merit for the departed and guiding them peacefully toward their next journey.

Encoffinment Rite 入殓

Monks chant as your loved one is laid into the casket, blessing the departed and sanctifying the beginning of the wake.

Nightly Chanting 诵经

Evening sutra recitations — commonly the Amitabha Sutra — transfer merit to the departed while bringing calm to the bereaved.

Funeral Day Service 出殡

The final-day rite includes prayers, last respects and the procession, conducted with solemnity before the cortege departs for cremation.

49 Days of Prayers 七七四十九天

In Buddhist belief, the consciousness journeys for up to 49 days. Weekly prayers, especially the 7th and 49th day, support a favourable rebirth.

Common Questions

Frequently asked

What should I do immediately when a loved one passes at home?

Call a doctor to certify the death (we can arrange one), then call our 24-hour hotline. Our team will take care of the transfer, documentation and all funeral arrangements from there.

Can the wake be held at an HDB void deck?

Yes. We obtain the necessary town council permit and handle the complete setup — tentage, lighting, altar, seating and nightly security — at the void deck, a funeral parlour, or your home.

Do you serve both Mahayana and Theravada traditions?

Yes. We work with venerable monks of both traditions and will arrange rites according to your family's practice and dialect group customs.

What happens after the cremation?

We accompany you for ash collection, then assist with your chosen resting place — a columbarium niche, sea scattering with Buddhist ritual, or other arrangements — plus the 7th-day, 49-day and 100-day prayers.
